# Moviet — концепты интерфейса

Три варианта для выбора визуального направления. Каждый показывает поиск и избранное. Данные фильмов, оценки и количество сохранённых фильмов демонстрационные.

Создано встроенным инструментом image_gen. Код приложения не менялся.

## 01-cinema

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a polished high-fidelity design concept board for the existing Android movie discovery app named Moviet. This app searches a movie database and lets users save favorites; it is not a streaming app. Show TWO complete large front-facing portrait Android app screens side by side on a landscape design presentation board, with readable UI and no perspective tilt, no hands, no desktop browser, no device marketing distractions. Phone screen proportions approximately 412:892, thin restrained rounded frames and small centered Android camera hole, complete status bars and gesture bars with safe padding. Left screen is movie search/discovery with a working-looking search field, movie poster imagery and ratings. Right is saved favorites, with a clear saved/bookmark state and search/favorites navigation. Russian UI, exact app name "Moviet", bottom navigation labels "Поиск" and "Избранное". Use fictional movie data consistently: "Орбита", "После дождя", "Тишина", "Ночной рейс", with distinctive cinematic photographic or illustrated posters. Metadata and ratings are illustrative mock data. No play buttons, subscription, purchases, streaming controls, profile or social features. Professional practical mobile UI, clear visual hierarchy, generous touch targets, crisp coherent icons and excellent typography, very high detail, no lorem ipsum, no gibberish, no watermarks. The two screens must belong to one coherent design system. Keep all UI in the frame. Direction 01: CINEMA. A premium immersive dark cinema aesthetic. Board background deep warm charcoal, restrained small heading outside phones "01 / CINEMA", subtitle "Тёмный · атмосферный". App backgrounds near-black #101114, soft charcoal surfaces, warm amber #FFBD69 accents, off-white typography. Left screen: simple Moviet wordmark, large heading "Найди своё кино", rounded search field "Фильм или сериал", compact genre filter chips "Все", "Фантастика", "Драма". A large dramatic cinematic featured card for "Орбита" with an astronaut on a lonely copper planet, subtle gradient behind a readable title, small rating badge and bookmark. Below heading "Популярное" and a small horizontal poster row of other movies. Right screen: heading "Избранное", small "12 фильмов" count, segmented "Все" / "Хочу посмотреть", a sophisticated two-column poster grid, titles and understated year lines below posters, amber saved bookmark corners. Bottom nav restrained and flat, active item amber. Strong cinematic photography, no gratuitous glow. This direction should feel calm, atmospheric and premium.
